Tips for Taking Photos

You'll need to provide photos from two angles:

  • Side:
  • Front:

Follow these tips to ensure your photos meet the requirements:

  • Use a mirror or a timer: Avoid angles or distortions, and ensure the camera is straight.
  • Capture from neck to knees: Provide a full-body view for verification (your face is not required).
  • Wear fitted clothing: Avoid loose clothing and do not send nude photos.
  • Ensure clarity and lighting: Photos should be clear, well-lit, and not blurry.
  • No additional subjects: Make sure you're the only person in the photo.
